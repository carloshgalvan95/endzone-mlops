    Pick hits follow the game card. The home win percent is rounded, 50 is a tie pick, and a final tie is a hit only when the pick is also a tie. Log loss uses finished games that have a winner and a market price. Both probabilities are clipped to 0.000001. Ties and games with no market price are left out of both log losses. Margin error is the mean absolute error of home score minus away score. Total error is the mean absolute error of the combined score. Both use the estimate that was published, on finished games only. Unfinished games stay out of every rate. Lower log loss is closer to the results. How this is counted.

    How to read each game

    The page compares the model with the market. The two cards are a frozen example from 2026 week 9. They are not the live slate.

    Every percent is for the home team.

    1. 1

      Teams. Away on top, home below. WINS is the model's pick. The home percent is rounded: above 50 picks the home team, below 50 picks the away team, and 50 shows TIE.

    2. 2

      Model. The model's probability that the home team wins.

    3. 3

      Market. The market's no-vig implied probability for the home team. After the game the line reads favored X · matched, favored X · did not match, or market even when that percent rounds to 50.

    4. 4

      Bars. Model on top, market below. The line in the center is 50%.

    5. 5

      Diff. Model minus market, in percentage points. Positive means the model likes the home team more than the market. A gap at least as large as the threshold shows DISAGREES. The default is 5 pp. The Threshold control changes it. The gap uses the absolute value.

    6. 6

      Before the game. Kickoff is in Mexico City time, labeled CDMX. The estimated score is away, then home, for example BUF 24 · 23 MIN. Points are rounded for display.

    7. 7

      Finished. The real score. means the pick matched the result, including a tie pick on a tie. means it missed. est. is the estimate published before kickoff. Final · date is the kickoff date in Mexico City.
